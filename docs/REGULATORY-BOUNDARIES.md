# Regulatory boundaries

The platform supports educational delivery and administrative preparation. It does not create state approval by itself.

## Texas training

- School/program approval and state credentialing remain controlled by the applicable Texas authority.
- TULIP-facing records use lifecycle states such as `draft`, `ready_for_transmittal`, `submitted`, `confirmed`, `rejected` and retain evidence.
- `submitted` requires an authorized submission event; `confirmed` requires return evidence.
- The public LMS must not expose unsupervised procedural medication-administration instructions that belong in supervised laboratory/clinical instruction.
- State equivalency outside Texas must be tracked per jurisdiction and may not be inferred automatically.

## AI

AI assistance can generate explanations, formative questions and bounded simulations. Instructor-authored objectives, approved curriculum, student records and competency decisions remain authoritative.
